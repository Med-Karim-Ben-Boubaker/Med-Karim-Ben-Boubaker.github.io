The directly evidenced mechanisms are evaluation, observability, failure handling, expert review, retrieval/graph grounding, and measured efficiency.

## 8. Positioning options and tradeoffs

### Option A — Evaluable knowledge systems for expert workflows (recommended)

> **AI Engineer building evaluable agent and knowledge systems for expert workflows.**

**Strengths:** Distinctive, extensible across legal, healthcare, and science; grounded in evaluation, graphs, retrieval, and deployment; does not overclaim domain authority.  
**Risks:** “Evaluable” is technical and needs a plain-language supporting sentence and visible proof.  
**Best use:** Primary headline and homepage structure.

### Option B — AI Engineer for regulated and expert domains

> **AI Engineer building agent systems for regulated and expert work.**

**Strengths:** More concrete and commercially legible; gives legal/compliance a meaningful place; still broader than legal tech.  
**Risks:** “Regulated” may imply legal/clinical authority, and the current healthcare work is research rather than validated clinical deployment.  
**Best use:** Supporting framing or a variant to test, not the sole identity yet.

### Option C — Legal-tech AI Engineer

> **Legal-tech AI Engineer building reliable knowledge systems.**

**Strengths:** Sharp and easy to understand for legal-tech employers; two professional experiences provide real evidence.  
**Risks:** Overfits a limited slice of the record, hides current healthcare/scientific research, and may imply deeper legal expertise than demonstrated.  
**Best use:** A legal/compliance project page or targeted job application, not the general homepage.

### Option D — Research-minded AI systems builder

> **Research-minded AI engineer building practical LLM systems.**

**Strengths:** Fits the current research role and technical writing; leaves room for future research.  
**Risks:** Less specific about value, knowledge grounding, and production engineering; “AI systems builder” alone is generic.  
**Best use:** Supporting descriptor in the About page or research-oriented variant.

## 9. Provisional recommendation

Use **Option A** as the primary direction, with legal/compliance as the first case study and healthcare/scientific research as the second. Keep “research-minded” as a supporting attribute and keep “reliable” as a concept that is demonstrated through evaluation, observability, failure handling, reviewability, and efficiency.

The user’s original idea is therefore **strategically right but linguistically overloaded**:

- Keep: AI Engineer, agents, knowledge graphs, domain-heavy work, legal/compliance proof.
- Reframe: “agent reliability” as evaluable/observable/testable systems.
- Replace: “domain/knowledge workers” with “expert workflows,” “knowledge-intensive products,” or a named audience when the target role is confirmed.
- Avoid: legal-tech as the permanent identity, unqualified “reliable,” clinical validation, legal expertise, explainability, and senior/staff signals.
