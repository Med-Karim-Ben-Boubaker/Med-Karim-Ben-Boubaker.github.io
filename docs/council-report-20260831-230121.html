Advisor responses

The Contrarian

The core value is converting messy domain knowledge into inspectable, testable systems. The proposed wording is too broad and jargon-heavy; legal tech is a proof domain, not yet a durable identity. Lead with research-minded AI engineering for reliable knowledge systems and use a plain-language subtitle.

The First Principles Thinker

The recurring capability is making LLM systems useful and controllable in regulated or expert workflows. “AI Engineer building reliable agents for knowledge-intensive work” is distinctive and extensible, while legal-tech is too narrow. The profile supports Applied AI or AI Systems Engineer scope, not senior/staff positioning.

The Expansionist

The durable opportunity is reliable AI knowledge systems: retrieval, graphs, agents, evaluation, observability, and deployment. Lead with regulated and expert work if reliability is demonstrated through concrete metrics, and test broad, regulated-domain, and legal-first hero variants.

The Outsider

“Domain/knowledge workers” is vague to a hiring manager. Lead with AI Engineer building reliable agents and knowledge systems for regulated work, but keep legal as an entry point and avoid expert, clinical-validation, explainability, or unsupported scale claims.

The Executor

Lead with reliable, evaluable agent systems for regulated and knowledge-heavy work, then support it with quantified proof: 79.4% fewer elicitation calls, 67% lower RAG latency, 30,000+ documents, and evaluation/observability infrastructure. First build a source-backed homepage brief.
